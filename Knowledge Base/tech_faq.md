# Tech FAQ

## How do I reset my password?
Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password". Enter your work email and you'll receive a reset link within 5 minutes.
If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact IT support at it-support@company.com.

## How do I set up VPN?
1. Download the VPN client from the IT portal (intranet > Tools > VPN).
2. Install and open the client.
3. Enter server: vpn.company.com
4. Log in with your company credentials.
5. Select the region closest to you for best performance.

## What is our tech stack?
- Frontend: React with TypeScript
- Backend: Python (FastAPI) and Node.js (Express)
- Database: PostgreSQL (primary), Redis (caching)
- Cloud: GCP (GCS, CloudRun, VertexAI, Cloud Composer)
- CI/CD: GitHub Actions
- Monitoring: Datadog + PagerDuty

## How do I request software licenses?
Submit a ticket via the IT portal under "Software Request". Include the software name, business justification, and number of seats needed. Approval typically takes 3–5 business days.

## Who do I contact for laptop issues?
Email it-support@company.com or walk into the IT helpdesk on Floor 2. Remote employees can use the remote support tool at support.company.com.
