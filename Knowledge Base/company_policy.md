# Company Policy

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Core hours are 10am–3pm local time.
All remote employees must be reachable via Teams during core hours.
Equipment requests must be submitted to IT at least 2 weeks in advance.

## Leave Policy
- Annual leave: 20 days per year, accrued monthly.
- Sick leave: 10 days per year, no carryover.
- Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid for primary carers, 4 weeks for secondary carers.
- Leave requests must be submitted via the HR portal with at least 2 weeks notice for planned leave.

## Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to treat colleagues with respect and professionalism.
Harassment or discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in immediate termination.
Any concerns should be reported to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline.

## Expense Policy
Expenses under $50 can be reimbursed with a receipt submitted via the expense portal.
Expenses over $50 require prior manager approval.
Business travel must be booked through the company travel portal.
